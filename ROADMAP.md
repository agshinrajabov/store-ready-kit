# Roadmap

v1.0 is exactly four skills: `store-audit`, `aso-research`, `store-assets`, `submission-pilot`. Ideas that come up
while building v1 are written here and stay out of v1.

## v1.1

- Google Play variants of all four skills.

## v1.2

- Pro decision gate: evaluated only if, within 90 days of v1.0, the repo reaches 500+ stars or 100+ email sign-ups.
  Candidates: hosted audit, monthly guidelines-update subscription, extended one-time pack. No monetisation work
  happens before the gate is met.

## Parking lot

- Compare competitor screenshots as images, not only as described concepts (store-audit, store-assets).
- A `guidelines_watch` check that flags when Apple publishes a new Review Guidelines revision.
- Per-niche template-trait catalogues beyond the three in v1 (finance, health, kids, productivity games).
- A demo mode in store-audit that runs the whole pipeline on the anonymised fixture, for the README recording.
