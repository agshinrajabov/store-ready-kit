Belayer helps climbers find a belay partner for a session tonight.

Demo account (full access, no expiry):
Username: review@belayer.app
Password: provided in the App Review Information field

How to see the core feature:
1. Sign in with the demo account.
2. Tap Tonight.
3. Tap any climber to open their profile and belay certification.
4. Tap Ask to belay; the demo account already has one accepted match to show the chat.

Matching needs two people, so the demo account comes with a pending and an accepted match.
