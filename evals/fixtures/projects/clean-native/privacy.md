# Lens — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Lens is an expense tracker. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## What we collect

- **Camera images.** When you photograph a receipt, the camera image is processed on your device to read the
  amount, date and merchant. The photo is stored only in your iCloud account; we never receive it.
- **Account details.** If you create an account with Sign in with Apple, we store the email address Apple gives
  us (which can be a private relay address) so we can sync your expenses.
- **Diagnostics.** Anonymous crash reports, not linked to you.

## What we do not do

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not sell data, and we show no ads.

## Retention and deletion

Delete your account from Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and synced data are deleted from our
servers within 30 days.

privacy@lens.example — we answer within five working days.
